# v4.7.2

基于 v4.7.1 的 Windows x64 修复版，包含 #84。Windows x64 fix release based on v4.7.1, covering #84.

## 中文

4.7.2 修复在线角色列表有时加载不出来的问题，功能与 4.7.1 相同。4.7 的全部新功能见 [v4.7.0 发布说明](v4.7.0.md)。

### 修复

- **原神、崩坏：星穹铁道、绝区零的角色列表有时加载不出来**：请求米游社 Wiki 时偶尔会卡住 20 秒以上，以前既没有超时也不会重试，角色栏只剩从 GameBanana 拼出来的英文名，按字母排序。现在角色目录用到的每个请求（米游社 Wiki、终末地官网、HoYoWiki、GameBanana 分类）最多等 20 秒，超时或网络出错时自动再试一次。
- **没填 Wiki 地址的仓库加载不出 Wiki 目录**：仓库没有可识别的 Wiki 地址、但对应原神、星铁或绝区零时，改用该游戏内置的 Wiki 地址。
- 重试和最终失败都会写进 `app-errors.log`，导出诊断报告时可以看到原因。

### 升级

- 4.7.1 及更早的正式版可以在软件内更新，也可以下载完整 ZIP；旧配置可以直接使用。
- 4.7.2 没有新功能，不会再显示“4.7 新增”的说明。

### 已知问题与验证边界

- 卡住的原因推断是米游社 CDN 轮换到连不上的节点，没能当场证实；如果更新后仍加载不出来，请在“设置 → 诊断与问题报告”导出诊断报告。
- 其余已知问题与 4.7.1 相同，见 [v4.7.1 发布说明](v4.7.1.md#已知问题与验证边界)。
- 第三方 Mod 与加载器可能导致账号处罚。发布包按 AGPL-3.0-only 分发，见 [许可证说明](../LICENSING.md)；验证结果见 [验证记录](../verification/v4.7.2.md)。

## English

4.7.2 fixes online character lists that sometimes failed to load; features are the same as 4.7.1. For everything new in 4.7 see the [v4.7.0 notes](v4.7.0.md).

### Fixes

- **Genshin Impact, Honkai: Star Rail and Zenless Zone Zero character lists sometimes didn't load:** the miHoYo wiki request occasionally stalled for 20 seconds or more, with no timeout and no retry, leaving only English names gathered from GameBanana in alphabetical order. Every request the character catalog uses (miHoYo wiki, Endfield site, HoYoWiki, GameBanana categories) now waits at most 20 seconds and is retried once after a timeout or network error.
- **Repositories without a wiki address never loaded a wiki catalog:** when a repository has no recognisable wiki address but belongs to Genshin, Star Rail or ZZZ, the game's built-in wiki address is used.
- Retries and final failures are written to `app-errors.log`, so they appear in diagnostic reports.

### Upgrade

- 4.7.1 and earlier stable releases can update in the app, or download the full ZIP. Existing configuration works as is.
- 4.7.2 has no new features, so the "New in 4.7" dialog is not shown again.

### Known issues and verification limits

- The stall is thought to come from miHoYo's CDN rotating to an unreachable node; this was not confirmed. If lists still don't load after updating, export a diagnostic report from Settings → Diagnostics and issue reports.
- The other known issues are the same as in 4.7.1; see the [v4.7.1 notes](v4.7.1.md#known-issues-and-verification-limits).
- Third-party Mods and loaders may lead to account penalties. The package is AGPL-3.0-only; see [LICENSING](../LICENSING.md). Results are in the [verification record](../verification/v4.7.2.md).
