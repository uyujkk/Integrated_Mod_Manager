# v4.8.4

基于 v4.8.3 的 Windows x64 正式版。Windows x64 stable release based on v4.8.3.

## 更新内容

用户反馈在线页的筛选状态和按钮用途不够明显，分类栏也不好用。这个版本据此调整了在线页（[#151](https://github.com/uyujkk/Integrated_Mod_Manager/pull/151)）。

- **正在用的筛选一目了然**：
  - 只要有筛选在起作用，搜索框下方就会出现一行：先是当前页的匹配数量（例如“当前页 1/2 个匹配”），然后每个筛选各一个标签：不是默认的分类、角色、作者、搜索、显示范围，以及不是按热度的排序。
  - 点标签上的 ✕ 只取消这一项。
  - “全部清除”回到默认列表：搜索、分类、角色、作者、显示范围和排序都恢复。
  - 没有筛选时这一行隐藏，底部只保留翻页。
- **按钮写明用途**：
  - 筛选按钮显示“显示：本页全部 ▾”，正在筛选时变为强调色。它沿用原来的“本页全部 / 本页已安装 / 本页已收藏”三项。
  - ☆ 和 ⚙ 按钮在空间足够时显示“作者与收藏”和“下载选项”，窗口较窄时只显示图标。
  - 排序不是按热度时，排序框上有一个小圆点。
  - 工具栏和翻页的每个按钮都有提示文字和读屏名称。以前这些按钮都没有。
- **分类栏重新排版**：
  - 子分类显示在一级分类下方，是一排带底色的标签，第一个是“全部”。
  - 一行放不下时才出现 ‹ › 箭头，也可以用鼠标滚轮滚动。
  - 窗口较窄时，一级分类收进工具栏里的“分类：X ▾”下拉菜单。
- **其他**：新文字有中文、英文和俄文。主程序、启动器、更新器和开发者工具使用 4.8.4。“新功能”对话框更新为 4.8.4 的内容，升级后显示一次。README 的在线页截图换成了新界面。

## 已知问题

- 新的在线页没有在高对比度主题下截图检查（检查需要更改系统设置）。页面只使用主题资源。
- 俄语界面只核对了文字，没有截图检查。
- 最窄的英文窗口里，排序框仍会截断成 “Sort by”，和以前一样。
- 4.8.3 列出的待核对项仍然有效：浮窗在真实游戏中发送 F10 和角色跟随，托盘图标自己的右键菜单和双击，用真实启动器“运行启动器”，用真实账号点赞。见 [v4.8.3 发布说明](v4.8.3.md#已知问题)。
- 真实游戏与加载器的其他验证范围见 [v4.8.0 发布说明](v4.8.0.md#尚未在真实游戏中核对的功能)。

## What's new

Users said the Online page's filter state and buttons were hard to read, and the category bar needed a better layout. This release reworks those parts of the Online page ([#151](https://github.com/uyujkk/Integrated_Mod_Manager/pull/151)).

- **Active filters at a glance**:
  - Whenever something narrows the list, a row appears under the search box. It shows the match count on the page ("This page: 1/2 matches"), then one chip for each filter: a category other than the default, character, author, search, the Show filter, and any sort other than hotness.
  - The ✕ on a chip removes only that filter.
  - **Clear all** goes back to the default list: search, category, character, author, the Show filter and the sort are all reset.
  - The row is hidden when nothing is filtered, and the footer keeps only paging.
- **Buttons say what they do**:
  - The filter button reads "Show: All on page ▾" and turns to the accent colour while it filters. It keeps the existing All / Installed / Saved on page choices.
  - The ☆ and ⚙ buttons read **Authors & saved** and **Download options** where there is room, and show only their icons in a narrow window.
  - The sort box shows a dot when it isn't sorting by hotness.
  - Every toolbar and paging button has a tooltip and an accessible name. Before this release, none of them did.
- **A tidier category bar**:
  - Subcategories sit under the top-level tabs as a row of filled chips, with **All** first.
  - ‹ › arrows appear only when the row doesn't fit, and the mouse wheel scrolls it.
  - In a narrow window the top-level categories fold into a **Category: X ▾** drop-down in the toolbar.
- **Other**: The new texts are in Chinese, English and Russian. The app, launcher, updater and Developer Tools use version 4.8.4. The What's New dialog describes 4.8.4 and shows once after upgrading. The README's Online page screenshot shows the new layout.

## Known issues

- The new Online page was not checked with screenshots in a high-contrast theme, because that needs a system setting changed. The page uses only theme resources.
- The Russian interface was checked through its texts only, without screenshots.
- In the narrowest English window, the sort box still cuts off at "Sort by", as before.
- The open checks listed for 4.8.3 still apply: the overlay sending F10 and following characters in a real game, the tray icon's own right-click menu and double-click, **Run launcher** with a real launcher, and liking with a real account. See the [v4.8.3 notes](v4.8.3.md#known-issues).
- See the [v4.8.0 notes](v4.8.0.md#not-yet-confirmed-in-a-real-game) for the scope of other game and loader verification.
